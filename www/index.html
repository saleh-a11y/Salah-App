    Backup

    Your prayers are saved only on this phone. Save a backup to Google Drive, WhatsApp or email so you can restore it on a new phone.

    Your qaḍā count is stored as a history of every change, so it is never lost. If “Back up to Google” is on in your phone’s settings, Android also keeps a copy of the app’s data.

    Ramaḍān mode

    Switches on by itself when Ramaḍān starts (Jamiatul Ulama dates). Turn it on now to see how it looks.

    Your privacy

    Everything you enter stays on this phone: your prayers, qaḍā, fasts and adhkār. No ads, no accounts, no tracking. The app only downloads masjid times, Jamiat's Hijri dates and update checks.
