# Salah Tracker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

Salah Tracker is a free prayer tracker for Android. It has **no ads, no accounts and no analytics or tracking**. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## What stays on your phone
Everything you enter stays **only on your phone**:
- the prayers you tick, late / jamāʿah / excused marks, Sunnah and nafl
- your qaḍā (missed prayer) estimate, make-up history and missed fasts
- your starting date, madhab and settings, your masjids
- adhkār counts and your own adhkār

The developer cannot see any of it. Nothing is sent to a server we run — there isn't one.

## Backups
- **Back up now** creates a file that *you* choose where to save (Google Drive, WhatsApp, email…). We never receive it.
- If "Back up to Google" is on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup. This is controlled by your phone and Google, not by us.

## Internet connections the app makes
The app only downloads public information. It does not upload your data.
- **Masjid boards** (masjidboardlive.com) — to read the prayer and jamāʿah times of the masjids you add.
- **Jamiatul Ulama South Africa** (jamiatsa.org) — to read the declared Islamic (Hijri) month dates.
- **GitHub** (github.com) — to check whether a new version of the app is available.

These sites receive the normal technical information any website receives (such as your IP address). Please see their own privacy policies.

## Location
Location is used **only** when you tap "Use my current location" in the Qibla compass, to work out the direction of the Kaʿbah. It is used on the phone and never stored or sent anywhere.

## Notifications and alarms
The app schedules prayer notifications and the adhān on your phone. Buttons on notifications (e.g. "Prayed") save the tick on your phone.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data
Settings → **Reset all data** erases everything, or uninstall the app. Backup files you saved yourself are yours to delete.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/saleh-a11y/Salah-App/issues
